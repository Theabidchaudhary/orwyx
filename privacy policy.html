Privacy Policy

What We Collect

Only what you actually give us.

When you get in touch, we collect the details you choose to share — your name, email address, and anything you tell us about your project. Beyond that, we use standard, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how the site is being used: pages visited, approximate region, device type. We never identify you personally through any of it.

How We Use It

To reply to you. Nothing else.

Your contact details exist for one reason — to respond to your inquiry and deliver the project you hired us for. We do not sell your information, rent it out, or share it with third parties for marketing. It goes nowhere we haven’t told you about. Full stop.

Embedded Video

Videos play privately, by design.

Portfolio videos on this site are embedded through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). No cookies are set by YouTube until you actively press play — and once you do, YouTube’s own privacy policy governs what happens from that point. We chose this setup deliberately so your visit stays as clean as possible.

Your Rights

It’s your data — you can take it back.

At any point, you can request a copy of any personal data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it entirely. No hoops, no runaround. Just email us at theabidchaudhary@gmail.com and we’ll handle it promptly.
