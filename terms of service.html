Terms of Service

Scope of Work

We agree on everything before we begin.

Every project kicks off with a written scope — deliverables, timeline, and a clear price. Nothing moves forward until we’re both signed off on it. If something new comes up mid-project that falls outside what we agreed, we’ll quote it separately and get your approval before any extra work starts. No hidden extras, no surprise invoices.

Revisions

Changes are welcome — within reason.

Your scope includes a set number of revision rounds, and we’ll use every one of them to get the result right. Revisions mean refining the agreed direction — adjusting cuts, pacing, colour, or copy within the original brief. If you want to explore a completely new creative direction, that’s a fresh scope. We’ll be upfront about it either way.

Ownership

Pay in full, own it outright.

Once your final invoice is settled, the finished deliverables are entirely yours — no licensing fees, no restrictions, no strings attached. We do reserve the right to showcase completed work in our portfolio and case studies, unless you’ve signed a confidentiality agreement with us. White-label clients are never credited, named, or displayed — your brand stays front and centre.

Your Materials

What you send us, you own the rights to.

By sharing footage, music, graphics, or any other assets with us, you confirm you hold the rights to use them. If we source licensed assets on your behalf — stock footage, music, fonts — we’ll make sure they’re licensed in your name or under terms that cover your intended use. We’ll flag anything that needs attention before it ends up in the final cut.

Questions

Something feel off? Just ask.

These terms are written to be clear, not to catch you out. If anything reads ambiguously or doesn’t make sense for your situation, reach out before we start — that’s always the best time to get aligned. Email us at theabidchaudhary@gmail.com and we’ll sort it out.
